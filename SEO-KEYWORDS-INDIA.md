# Keyword Architecture - Edwin Leathers (India-First)

**Date:** August 2026
**Ground rules applied:**
- **No fabricated search volumes.** Nothing below invents numbers. Priority is by commercial logic, not fake data.
- **Confidence** = how sure we are the opportunity exists given the catalog + business. "Requires catalog check" means the seeded catalog (Bags, Wallets, Belts, Travel, Accessories, Work) doesn't yet show that category - confirm the live MongoDB catalog first.
- **No city spam.** South-India queries are listed as hypotheses that must be validated in Search Console before any dedicated pages are built.
- Every keyword maps to a landing page that already exists or can be built with genuine content.

**How to read:** *Intent* = what the searcher wants (Buy / Compare / Learn). *Landing page* = where we should send them. *Commercial importance* = likely effect on orders/revenue. *Confidence* = how sure we are of the opportunity.

## How to turn this into work

1. **Now (free):** categories + product pages now exist and rank targets are set. Publish 3-4 guides in the next month.
2. **After GSC data (~6-8 weeks):** validate the South-India hypotheses; decide on the 2-3 location pages that earn their place.
3. **When the catalog grows:** add Shoes/Boots/Women's categories as real inventory appears - each becomes a new Tier-1/2 landing page. Do not create empty category shells just for keywords.
4. **Revisit monthly:** pull GSC queries containing leather/bags/ladies/wallets/belts/office/laptop/shoes, note positions 4-20, and feed winning patterns back into titles/descriptions.
